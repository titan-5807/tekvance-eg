Who We Are

Your Trusted Technology Partner

TEKVANCE is a technology solutions company specializing in IT infrastructure, networking, CCTV & security systems, server & data center solutions, and IP telephony. We combine deep technical expertise with a hands-on approach to deliver systems that are reliable today and ready for tomorrow.
